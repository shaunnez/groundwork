# Groundwork private pilot: Bobby's review

Website: https://groundwork-production-fcaa.up.railway.app

Use the separate reviewer access key Shaun shares privately. Nothing needs to run on your computer. This is the original Groundwork interface connected to a working Node.js/TypeScript backend, Postgres database and persistent evidence storage.

## What to test

1. **Home and Watchlist:** open the saved opportunities. Check whether the summaries make the next action clear and distinguish real public evidence, synthetic examples and unassessed notices.
2. **Pursuit package:** open **CSP26317 – Psychometric testing**. This real public notice records a direct award, not an open invitation to bid. Review the executive summary, centre of gravity, cone of plausibility, competing hypotheses, risk register, limitations and recommendation. Tell Shaun where the depth or reasoning fails to help a bid decision.
3. **Evidence:** click the source references. Check that they open the relevant original text, and that a supported fact is distinguishable from an assessment or an information gap.
4. **RFP reassessment:** open the labelled **synthetic Harbour** example. Compare its baseline and reassessment, inspect the mandatory requirement in section 18 and the deadline change from 5 to 12 October 2026. This tests the workflow; it does not validate a real tender.
5. **Other reports:** inspect the saved daily watchlist, competitor profiles and weekly briefs. Check whether they tell you something useful without overstating supplier identity, incumbency or evidence coverage.
6. **Feedback and decisions:** record an issue or correction and check it persists after reloading. Keep a firm's bid decision separate from an analyst's assessment. Mark test entries clearly, especially on the synthetic case.

For each issue, record the opportunity/report, the section, what is wrong, what you expected and (where relevant) the source that supports the correction. Screenshots help with layout problems.

## Current access and limits

Reviewer access supports reading reports/evidence, feedback and decisions. Shaun's owner access manages evidence intake, configuration and generation. The hosted official Claude Code login uses Shaun's own subscription for owner runs; your reviewer key does not borrow that subscription for your own generation.

Public and synthetic examples are deliberately labelled. The original evidence behind Bobby's earlier example has not been supplied, so that example has not been independently validated. Reports remain internal drafts requiring analyst review; customer publication, external delivery, automated public research and RealMe/GETS integration are not enabled. Explicit owner-triggered Firecrawl searches are enabled within the original included-credit allowance; results remain leads until a permitted source is added to the evidence pack.
