## Boundaries and incomplete launch work

This is local internal functionality, not customer launch. Discovery is bounded search returning unverified leads. Admitting a source remains an explicit permitted acquisition step. A scheduled refresh compiles the latest accepted snapshot, reports new-source staleness and may run an explicitly configured bounded discovery query; it does not silently scrape leads, invent a new cutoff, approve reports or send externally. No recurring desktop automation is installed.

Competitor profiles show actual named entities, quoted relations, notice-linked facts, award-population context, positioning and collection gaps. Their depth depends on admitted evidence. The public case has little capability evidence; it is not a fully researched supplier dossier or a market-wide award history. Account/client daily and weekly collections combine immutable notice-level outputs, retaining each cutoff, provenance and review status. Unassessed notices remain visible and block complete collection delivery. Collections do not invent cross-notice market trends or equate similarly named suppliers.
